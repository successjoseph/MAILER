Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

At MAILER, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI Email Assistant service. We built MAILER with a "Privacy First" architecture, ensuring minimal data retention.

2. Data We Collect

Google Account Information: When you connect your Gmail account via Google OAuth, we receive your email address and profile information to identify you.

Email Data: Our system accesses your unread emails to generate draft replies. We do NOT store the full body of your emails permanently. We transiently process the email content through our AI models (Llama-3.3-70b) and store only a brief summary of the interaction for your "Daily Report."

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To draft replies to your unread emails based on your defined "Manifesto" (persona).
  • To provide you with summaries of AI activity.
  • To improve our AI response quality (only with anonymized data, if you opt-in).

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security protocols. Since we rely on LLMs, we ensure that data sent to inference providers is done so under strict data processing agreements that prevent them from training on your personal data.

5. Your Controls

You can revoke MAILER's access to your Gmail account at any time via your Google Security settings or within the MAILER dashboard. You can also delete your account, which will wipe all summaries and configuration data from our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@projectmailer.com.
